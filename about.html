Our Mission

ClarixNews was created with a straightforward goal: make useful, readable news and information available online without a paywall. The publication covers international affairs, politics, business, technology, markets, weather and career-related topics.

We aim to separate reporting from advertising, make corrections when errors are identified, and give readers enough context to understand a story rather than simply presenting a headline.

Editorial Team

ClarixNews is a digital publication project. Editorial bylines and contributor information are shown on individual articles where applicable. We do not present a fictional staff directory as a list of real employees.

For questions about a particular article, source, byline or correction, please use the Contact page.

Advertising & Partnerships

ClarixNews uses advertising to support the website. Advertising and editorial content are intended to remain separate. Commercial relationships should not determine the substance of editorial coverage.

If you are interested in advertising or a partnership, please visit our contact page.

Corrections Policy

We take accuracy seriously. If you believe an article contains a factual error, please contact us with the article URL, the specific issue and supporting information where possible.

After review, verified corrections may be updated in the relevant article. For correction requests, use the Contact page.
