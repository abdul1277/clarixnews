Welcome to ClarixNews (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

Depending on the features you use, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name and email address when contacting us, submitting a comment, or subscribing to a newsletter.

Information Collected Automatically

Our website and third-party services may collect technical and usage information such as IP address, approximate location, browser/device information, pages viewed, referring pages, and timestamps. The exact information collected depends on the service and your browser settings.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and improve website functionality and content.
  • Respond to messages, tips, correction requests, and support requests.
  • Operate newsletter or communication features when available and requested.
  • Understand website traffic and usage through analytics.
  • Support advertising and measure advertising performance.
  • Help detect abuse, fraud, and security problems.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing. Third-party providers may process information as part of the services they provide to the site.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

ClarixNews and third-party services may use cookies, local storage, pixels, or similar technologies. These technologies can remember preferences, measure traffic, support advertising, and help maintain site functionality.

  • Essential functionality: preferences and features needed for the site to operate.
  • Analytics: measurement of visits and site usage, including Google Analytics.
  • Advertising: advertising measurement and personalization where supported by Google and its partners.

You can control cookies through browser settings. Some third-party advertising choices are also available through Google's advertising controls. Your choices and the technologies used may vary by browser, region, and consent settings.

4. Advertising

ClarixNews may use Google AdSense and related Google advertising technologies. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, personalize, measure, or limit advertisements. ClarixNews does not control the individual cookies or processing performed by third-party advertising providers.

For current information about Google's advertising practices and available controls, please review Google's own privacy and advertising documentation.

5. Third-Party Services

Depending on the features active on the website, third-party services may include:

  • Google Analytics — website analytics.
  • Google AdSense — advertising.
  • Google Fonts — web fonts.
  • Vercel — hosting and deployment.

These providers have their own policies and may process information under their respective terms. This policy describes ClarixNews's use of these services at a general level and does not replace their policies.

6. Data Retention

Retention depends on the purpose and service involved. We aim to keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legal requirements, dispute resolution, and legitimate operational needs. Where a specific retention period is not stated, no fixed period is promised by this policy.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent. You may also unsubscribe from communications where an unsubscribe option is provided.

To make a privacy request, use our Contact page and clearly describe your request. We may need reasonable information to verify and process a request.

8. Children's Privacy

ClarixNews is not specifically directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the site, please contact us so the information can be reviewed and, where appropriate, removed.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services, practices, or legal requirements change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page. Please review this page periodically for updates.
